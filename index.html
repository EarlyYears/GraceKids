How incident reports work
  1. Log it. Fill in volunteer name, search for the child, describe what happened and any action taken, click Log incident. It is now saved as Pending.
  2. At Pickup. The child with the incident is flagged. They cannot be checked out until the incident is resolved.
  3. To Resolve. From the Pending list above (or the Resolve button in the checkout popup), click Resolve. Confirm the parents-informed status, review the email body, click Send & Clear.
  4. Edit or Discard if needed. Edit reloads the pending report into the form for revision. Discard deletes it without sending — use only if logged in error.
Note: parents must have an email on file in the Registry. No email = cannot send.
